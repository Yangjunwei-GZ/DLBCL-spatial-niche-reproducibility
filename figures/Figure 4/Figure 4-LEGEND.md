# Figure 4 | External replication of the six-program continuous architecture in independent DLBCL cohorts

**A,** Pearson correlation matrices for the six standardized continuous programs in GSE10846 (n = 420) and GSE181063 (n = 1,310). Program order was fixed according to the discovery analysis and was not reclustered within either replication cohort.

**B,** Comparison of the 15 nonredundant pairwise program correlations in GSE31312 with the corresponding correlations in GSE10846 and GSE181063. Each point represents one program pair, and diagonal lines indicate equality between discovery and replication estimates. The 15 edges were defined using pairwise Pearson correlations within each cohort; rank-order concordance between the GSE31312 edge vector and each external-cohort edge vector was assessed using Spearman correlation. The directions of 12 of 15 and 13 of 15 edges were concordant in GSE10846 and GSE181063, respectively.

**C,** Cross-cohort profile of all 15 pairwise Pearson correlations in GSE31312, GSE10846, and GSE181063. Rows were ordered by the corresponding full-precision GSE31312 correlation for visualization only.

Overall, the six-program correlation architecture showed strong rank-order concordance across the independent cohorts, although a small number of weak correlations differed in direction. Program order was fixed, no matrix was reclustered, no ecosystem classes or centroid projection were used, and no taxonomy was assigned to external samples.

MR, Macrophage-rich; TCI, T cell-inflamed; AP, Antigen-presentation; SF, Stromal/fibrotic; IC/EX, Immune-cold/exclusion; PC, Proliferative/cycling.
