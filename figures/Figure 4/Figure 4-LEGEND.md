
Figure 4 | External replication with prespecified reviewer sensitivities.

(A-C) Historical primary 15-edge analysis, preserved unchanged: GSE10846 n=420 full set, Spearman rho=0.936; GSE181063 rho=0.982. (D) Frozen reviewer-sensitivity results. Excluding the 2 shared-gene edges and 5 proliferation edges yielded rho=0.690 for GSE10846. Harmonized full scoring yielded rho=0.957 for GSE10846 and 0.975 for GSE181063; de-overlap yielded 0.946 and 0.950; common-detected-gene scoring yielded 0.950 and 0.979. Reviewer scoring sensitivities use the n=414 unsorted-bulk GSE10846 set; the n=420 full set remains the historical primary and was separately audited. Historical GSE10846 absolute agreement with GSE31312 had mean |delta r|=0.154 and maximum |delta r|=0.438. Rank preservation remained substantial under harmonized de-overlap and common-detected-gene sensitivities, while absolute edge differences and the edge-exclusion sensitivity qualify the strength of replication. Values are frozen and rounded only for display.
