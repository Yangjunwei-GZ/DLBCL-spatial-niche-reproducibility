Supplementary Fig. S4 | Canonical UCell spatial distributions across five primary DLBCL capture areas.

Supplementary Fig. S4 extends the representative spatial display in the main text by showing all five primary DLBCL capture areas scored with the frozen canonical UCell framework. Rows correspond to Cap.area3, Cap.area4, Cap.area5, Cap.area6, and Cap.area7; columns correspond to macrophage-rich (MR), T cell-inflamed (TCI), antigen-presentation (AP), stromal/fibrotic (SF), immune-cold/exclusion (IC/EX), and proliferative/cycling (PC) continuous programs. Frozen spot-level canonical UCell scores and frozen 10x spatial coordinates were used without rescoring, spot filtering, flipping, mirroring, or realignment. For each program, the color scale is shared across all five areas and uses the pooled 1st and 99th percentiles as display-only limits; values outside these limits are clipped for color rendering only and remain unchanged in the source data.

E denotes an exploratory-only antigen-presentation display. Antigen-presentation maps in Cap.area4-Cap.area7 are retained for transparency but are not evidence-equivalent to primary-eligible panels because only 13/22 canonical genes were detected in those areas. The Cap.area3 antigen-presentation map was primary-eligible with 21/22 detected canonical genes. No discrete taxonomy was assigned, and no biological or spatial-statistical analysis was rerun for this supplementary display.
