Supplementary Figure S2 | Extended diagnostics for candidate-k model adjudication

**A,** Consensus matrices for candidate partitions k=2-6, shown in their recorded consensus order on a common 0-1 scale. Neutral identifiers C1-Ck denote plotting partitions only and are not biological classes. **B,** Consensus cumulative distribution function (CDF) curves for k=2-6. **C,** CDF area and delta-area diagnostics; delta area for k=2 is structurally undefined and is displayed as not available without imputation. **D,** PAC, mean silhouette, minimum cluster size, minimum-cluster median Jaccard, and overall median Jaccard. **E,** Evaluation against six retention criteria: PAC rank no greater than 2, mean silhouette greater than 0.25, silhouette within 0.02 of the best value in the same score space, minimum cluster size of at least 25, minimum-cluster median Jaccard of at least 0.60, and overall median Jaccard of at least 0.75. Candidate k=2, 3, 4, 5, and 6 failed 3, 5, 6, 5, and 5 criteria, respectively. No candidate k satisfied all retention criteria; therefore, final k was not selected and no discrete taxonomy was assigned.
