Supplementary Fig. S3 | Single-cell cohort composition and frozen annotation structure in GSE182434.

(A) Cohort boundary for the full GSE182434 dataset (28,416 cells), comprising DLBCL (14,368 cells), follicular lymphoma (10,219 cells), and tonsil (3,829 cells). Primary Figure 5 analyses were restricted to the DLBCL component (14,368 cells from four patients and eight samples). (B) Descriptive cell counts across the four DLBCL patients, with bars partitioned by B-cell-sorted (B) and non-B-cell-sorted (NB) samples. (C) Composition of the DLBCL subset according to the nine frozen CellType annotations, shown as cell counts and percentages of the 14,368-cell subset. (D) Frozen CellType composition across patients. Colors denote within-patient proportions, and numerical labels report raw cell counts.

Patient, sample, and CellType counts are descriptive; cells are nested within patients and samples. Frozen CellType annotations were retained unchanged. No reannotation, marker discovery, program rescoring, or patient-level inferential testing was performed for this figure.
