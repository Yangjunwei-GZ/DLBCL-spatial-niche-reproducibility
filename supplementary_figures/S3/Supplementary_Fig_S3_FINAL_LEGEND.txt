Supplementary Figure S3 | Single-cell cohort composition and annotation structure in GSE182434

**A,** Cohort boundary for the full GSE182434 dataset (28,416 cells), comprising DLBCL (14,368 cells), follicular lymphoma (10,219 cells), and tonsil (3,829 cells). Primary Figure 5 analyses were restricted to the DLBCL component (14,368 cells from four patients and eight samples). **B,** Descriptive cell counts across the four DLBCL patients, partitioned by B-cell-sorted and non-B-cell-sorted samples. **C,** Composition of the DLBCL subset according to nine retained CellType annotations, shown as cell counts and percentages of the 14,368-cell subset. **D,** CellType composition across patients. Colors denote within-patient proportions, and labels report raw cell counts. Cells are nested within patients and samples; no reannotation, marker discovery, program rescoring, or patient-level inferential test was performed for this figure.
