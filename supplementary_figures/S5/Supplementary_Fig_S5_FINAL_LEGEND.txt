Supplementary Figure S5 | Spatial sensitivity analyses and their denominators

The panels summarize three distinct analysis families. **Matched random-gene-set null:** support at both Moran's I and Geary's C endpoints occurred for 42/54 evaluable capture area-by-program combinations overall and 25/32 primary-plus-exploratory combinations. Unsupported combinations include Cap.area3 by Antigen-presentation (coverage 21/22; Moran standardized effect -0.201; empirical P=0.593), Cap.area5 by Stromal/fibrotic, Cap.area7 by Stromal/fibrotic, and Cap.area7 by Immune-cold/exclusion. **Depth-adjusted residual analysis:** 30/30 DLBCL-area combinations supported both residual spatial endpoints. This is a separate sensitivity analysis and does not replace unsupported matched-null results. **Method consistency:** 108 pairwise method comparisons had a median Spearman rho of 0.878; 18/108 were below 0.7, 4/108 were below 0.5, and the minimum was 0.190. Seventeen of the 18 values below 0.7 involved ssGSEA; the remaining value was Cap.area3 by Antigen-presentation, primary versus SCTransform/UCell (rho=0.673). All denominators refer to the analysis family stated and are not interchangeable.
