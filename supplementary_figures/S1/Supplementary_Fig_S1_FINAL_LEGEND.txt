Supplementary Figure S1 | Program-gene coverage across datasets and primary DLBCL spatial areas

This coverage and quality-control figure summarizes six continuous programs: Macrophage-rich, T cell-inflamed, Antigen-presentation, Stromal/fibrotic, Immune-cold/exclusion, and Proliferative/cycling. Each program contains 22 memberships, for 132 memberships in total. These represent 121 unique genes because 11 genes occur in two programs.

**A,** Program-level gene coverage in GSE31312 discovery bulk transcriptomes, GSE10846 and GSE181063 external bulk cohorts, and GSE182434 single-cell contextualization. **B,** Dataset-level totals were 132/132 (100.0%) for GSE31312, 128/132 (97.0%) for GSE10846, 126/132 (95.5%) for GSE181063, and 132/132 (100.0%) for GSE182434. **C,** Program-gene coverage across Cap.area3-Cap.area7, the five primary DLBCL capture areas. Eligibility was defined as at least 16/22 genes for primary analysis, 12-15/22 for exploratory analysis, and fewer than 12/22 for non-evaluable combinations. Antigen-presentation coverage was 21/22 in Cap.area3 and 13/22 in Cap.area4-Cap.area7; the latter combinations were retained as coverage-limited exploratory analyses. No missing gene was substituted or imputed. The panel reports coverage only and does not constitute a new biological analysis.
