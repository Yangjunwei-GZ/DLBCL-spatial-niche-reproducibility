Supplementary Figure S6 | Spatial expression support for curated ligand-receptor axes

**A,** Ten curated axes were assessed across five primary DLBCL capture areas, yielding 50 possible area-by-axis evaluations; 45 were evaluable under the analysis-defined availability rule. No current continuous-program ligand-receptor association was generated. **B,** Same-spot Spearman correlations between ligand and receptor expression. Benjamini-Hochberg FDR values were retained for the family of 45 evaluable area-by-axis comparisons; asterisks denote FDR below 0.05. Positive, negative, and nonsignificant results are shown. **C,** k-nearest-neighbor neighborhood expression support using k=6 and 1,000 permutations. Color denotes log2 enrichment, dot size denotes -log10 BH FDR, and asterisks denote FDR below 0.05 across the same 45 evaluable comparisons. **D,** Separate ligand and receptor expression maps for MIF-CD74 and CXCL9-CXCR3 in GSM8500537_Cap.area4_DLBCL_V2. Maps use normalized gene-expression columns and do not display a composite ligand-receptor score. Not evaluable indicates that a required ligand or receptor gene was unavailable; it does not indicate absent signaling or a nonsignificant result. Same-spot expression compatibility does not demonstrate same-cell interaction, and neighborhood support reflects proximity rather than physical contact. These analyses do not establish direct signaling, receptor activation, functional communication, or causality.
